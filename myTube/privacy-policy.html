Privacy Policy

MyTube - Personal YouTube Channel Manager

Last Updated: January 2025

Your Privacy Matters: MyTube is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MyTube ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how MyTube collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using MyTube, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you use MyTube, we collect the following personal information:

  • Email Address: Used for account authentication and identification
  • Password: Encrypted and stored securely through Firebase Authentication
  • User ID (UID): Automatically generated unique identifier for your account

2.2 Usage Data

We collect information about how you interact with the App:

  • YouTube Channels: List of YouTube channels you add to your personal collection
  • Channel Information: Channel names, URLs, subscriber counts, and profile pictures
  • App Activity: Login times, channel additions/deletions, and video viewing history

2.3 Technical Data

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version and performance data
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • Network connection information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and provide secure access to your account
  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve the MyTube app functionality
  • Data Storage: To store your personalized YouTube channel lists in your private database space
  • Communication: To send important updates, security alerts, and support responses
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the app and improve user experience
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Your Data is Stored

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Stores encrypted credentials
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Stores your channel lists under your unique user ID (users/{uid}/channels)
  • Local Cache: Temporary storage on your device for faster app performance

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for authentication
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Firebase security rules preventing unauthorized access
  • User-specific database paths ensuring data isolation
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Admin-controlled user account creation

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do NOT Share Your Data

MyTube does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is private and isolated to your account only.

5.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may have access to certain data:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication and database services (covered by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube Data API: To fetch public channel information (covered by Google's Privacy Policy)

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests, such as:

  • Court orders or subpoenas
  • Legal processes or government requests
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevention of fraud or security threats

6. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data stored in MyTube
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Request an export of your channel lists and data
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication credentials are immediately removed
  • Your channel lists and preferences are permanently deleted
  • Cached data is cleared from our systems
  • Backup data is removed within 30 days

8. Children's Privacy

MyTube is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information.

9. Cookies and Tracking

MyTube uses minimal tracking technologies:

  • Session Tokens: To maintain your login state
  • Local Storage: To cache channel information for faster loading
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics to improve the app

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using MyTube, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Sending an email notification to your registered email address
  • Displaying a prominent notice in the app

Your continued use of MyTube after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

12. Your Consent

By using MyTube, you consent to:

  • The collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy
  • The storage of your data on Firebase servers
  • The use of YouTube Data API to fetch channel information
  • The processing of your data for app functionality and improvements

13. Data Protection Officer

For any privacy-related concerns or questions, you can contact our Data Protection Officer at the email address provided below.

14. Compliance

MyTube complies with applicable data protection laws and regulations, including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU users
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents
  • Other applicable regional data protection laws
