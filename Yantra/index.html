Powerful Features

Everything you need in one app

Professional Mouse

Precision touchpad with left, right, middle click and smooth scrolling. Adjustable sensitivity for perfect control.

Full Keyboard

Complete QWERTY layout with F-keys, modifiers, and navigation keys. All shortcuts work perfectly (Ctrl+C, Alt+Tab, Win+R).

Bluetooth HID

Direct Bluetooth connection using HID protocol. No server or receiver needed. Works with any computer.

Dual Orientation

Optimized layouts for both portrait and landscape modes. Compact keyboard in vertical, full layout in horizontal.

Instant Switching

Switch between mouse and keyboard modes instantly without reconnecting. Seamless workflow.

Haptic Feedback

Native vibration on every key press. Light feedback for regular keys, stronger for modifiers.

How It Works

Get started in 3 simple steps

Pair Your Device

Open Bluetooth settings on your computer and pair your Android phone. Grant Bluetooth permissions in the app.

Connect

Open Yantra app, select your computer from the paired devices list, and tap to connect. Connection is instant!

Start Using

Use the touchpad to move your cursor, click buttons, or switch to keyboard mode. It's that simple!
