Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2025

Complete Privacy: Subrosa Stories is a client-side only app. The developer has NO access to your data, NO backend servers, and NO data collection. You control everything - your API key, your stories, your data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Subrosa Stories, an AI-powered creative storytelling application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app works and clarifies that the developer does not collect, access, or store any of your data. Subrosa Stories is a completely client-side application with no backend infrastructure, no user accounts, and no data transmission to the developer.

2. What the Developer Does NOT Collect

The developer of Subrosa Stories does not collect, access, receive, or store ANY of your data. This includes:

  • Your Google Gemini API key
  • Your stories, prompts, or generated content
  • Your app settings or preferences
  • Your device information or identifiers
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Any personal information whatsoever

3. How the App Works (Client-Side Only)

Subrosa Stories operates entirely on your device:

  • Your API Key: You obtain your own Google Gemini API key from Google. You enter it into the app, and it's stored locally on your device only.
  • Story Generation: When you create a story, your device communicates directly with Google's Gemini API using YOUR API key. The developer is not involved in this process.
  • Story Storage: All stories are saved locally on your device. They never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.
  • No Backend: There are no developer-owned servers, databases, or cloud services involved.

4. How Your Data is Used (On Your Device Only)

All data processing happens locally on your device:

  • Your API key is used to authenticate with Google's Gemini API
  • Your stories are saved in your device's local storage
  • Your preferences are stored locally to remember your settings
  • Export and share features use your device's native capabilities

Important: The developer has no access to any of this data.

5. Data Storage (Local Only)

All data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • API Key: Stored in encrypted local storage on your device
  • Stories: Saved in your device's local database
  • Settings: Stored in local preferences
  • No Cloud Sync: Nothing is synced to any cloud or server
  • No Developer Access: The developer cannot access your device's local storage

6. Communication with Google Gemini API

When you generate a story, the app communicates directly with Google's Gemini API:

  • Direct Connection: Your device connects directly to Google's servers, not through any developer-owned servers
  • Your API Key: You use your own Google Gemini API key, which you obtain from Google
  • Your Responsibility: You are responsible for your API key and any usage charges from Google
  • Google's Privacy Policy: Data sent to Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Developer Not Involved: The developer does not see, log, or store any of this communication

7. No Third-Party Services (Except Google Gemini)

Subrosa Stories does not use any third-party services except for Google Gemini API (which YOU connect to directly):

  • No analytics or tracking services
  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting services
  • No cloud storage or backup services
  • No social media integrations
  • No developer-owned backend or servers

8. Data Sharing

The developer does not share your data because the developer does not have access to your data.

Your data is only shared when YOU choose to:

  • Send prompts to Google Gemini API (using your own API key)
  • Export stories as PDF or text files
  • Share stories using your device's share functionality

The developer is not involved in any of these actions.

9. Your Complete Control

You have 100% control over your data because everything is on your device:

  • Your API Key: You obtain it from Google, you enter it, you can change or remove it anytime
  • Your Stories: Stored only on your device, delete them anytime
  • Your Settings: Change or reset them anytime
  • Complete Removal: Uninstall the app to remove all data from your device
  • No Developer Involvement: The developer cannot access, view, or delete your data

12. Children's Privacy

Since the developer does not collect any data from anyone (including children), there is no children's data to protect. However, parents should be aware that:

  • Users need to obtain their own Google Gemini API key
  • API usage may incur charges from Google
  • Generated content depends on user prompts and AI responses

10. Data Retention

Data is retained only on your device until you delete it:

  • Delete individual stories through the app
  • Clear all history from app settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data
  • Clear app data through device settings

The developer does not retain any data because the developer does not have access to any data.

11. Security

Your data security depends on your device:

  • API keys are stored in encrypted local storage
  • Communication with Google uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • No data is sent to developer servers (because there are none)
  • Your device's security features protect your local data
  • Keep your device secure with a lock screen and regular updates

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

14. International Users

Subrosa Stories can be used worldwide. However, please note that:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • API communications with Google Gemini are subject to Google's data handling practices
  • You are responsible for ensuring your use complies with local laws
