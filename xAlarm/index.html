About xAlarm

xAlarm ensures you wake up on time, every time, with reliable alarm triggering and unique action-based dismissal methods to defeat even the heaviest sleeper.

Multiple Alarm Types

Create one-time alarms for special occasions or recurring alarms for specific days of the week, with independent customization options.

Smart Dismissal Actions

Choose from math problems, memory games, phone shaking, barcode scanning, photo capture, or physical activity to dismiss your alarm.

Reliability Focused

Multiple fallback mechanisms ensure alarms trigger reliably even on devices with aggressive battery optimization.
