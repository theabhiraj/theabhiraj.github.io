Privacy Policy

Number Quest: Puzzle App

Developer: Abhiraj Rathod

App Type: Free Puzzle Game for All Ages (3–100)

Your Privacy Matters

Number Quest is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our simple approach to data and privacy.

We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.

What We Don't Collect

  • No names or personal identifiers
  • No email addresses or phone numbers
  • No location data or GPS information
  • No browsing history or usage patterns
  • No device information beyond what's required for the app to function

How the App Works

  • The app runs completely offline on your device
  • All puzzle progress and settings are stored locally on your device only
  • Basic app store services (installation and updates) are handled by your device's app store
  • No third-party advertising networks are used
  • No analytics or tracking services are currently implemented

Data Security

Since Number Quest does not collect any personal information, there is no risk of your data being misused, shared, or compromised. Your puzzle game experience remains entirely private and secure on your device.

Effective Date: 08 April 2025

Future Updates

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this policy and notify users through the app. Any changes will always prioritize your privacy and security.
