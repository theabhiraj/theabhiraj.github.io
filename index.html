Subrosa Stories: AI-Powered Creative Storytelling

Unleash Your Creativity with AI

Subrosa Stories is a creative AI-powered story generation app built with Flutter that helps users create unique narratives across various genres, from Horror to Comedy and everything in between.

Multiple Narrative Styles

Choose from 8 different genres including Horror, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Romance, Mystery, Historical, Comedy, or Default for a unique storytelling experience every time.

Customizable Parameters

Fine-tune your story with adjustable creativity levels (0.1-1.0) and length options from short (~150 words) to long (~600 words) to suit your preference.

Export & Share Options

Save your stories as text files, export as professionally formatted PDFs, or share directly from the app with the integrated system share sheet.

Ready to create your own stories?

Subrosa Stories combines the power of Google's Gemini AI with an intuitive Flutter interface to help you generate unique, creative stories in seconds. With multiple narrative styles, customizable parameters, and easy export options, it's perfect for writers, students, or anyone who enjoys storytelling.
