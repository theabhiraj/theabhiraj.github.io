Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2025

Privacy First: TTKid is designed with children's privacy and safety as our top priority. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

1. Introduction

Welcome to TTKid, an educational learning app designed for toddlers aged 2-3 years. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our app. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

TTKid is designed to be completely private and safe for children. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history or app usage analytics
  • Contact lists or social media information
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. Local Data Storage

TTKid stores minimal data locally on your device to enhance the learning experience. This data never leaves your device and includes:

  • Learning Progress: Tracks which letters, numbers, and animals your child has completed
  • Game Scores: Saves high scores and achievements in games
  • App Settings: Stores preferences like sound volume and TTS settings

All this data is stored locally using the device's secure storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS) and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

4. No Internet Connection Required

TTKid works completely offline. The app does not require an internet connection to function, and no data is transmitted over the internet. All features, games, and learning content are available offline.

5. No Third-Party Services

TTKid does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services
  • No cloud storage services

6. Permissions

TTKid requests minimal permissions to function properly:

Android Permissions:

  • Storage: To save learning progress and game data locally on your device
  • Audio: To play educational sounds, music, and text-to-speech

iOS Permissions:

  • Local Storage: To save app settings and progress
  • Audio: To play sounds and enable text-to-speech features

These permissions are used solely for app functionality and do not enable any data collection or transmission.

7. Children's Privacy

TTKid is specifically designed for children aged 2-3 years. We take children's privacy extremely seriously:

  • No personal information is collected from children
  • No registration or account creation is required
  • No communication features (chat, messaging, etc.)
  • No external links or web browsers
  • No advertisements or in-app purchases
  • No social sharing features

8. Parental Control

Parents have complete control over the app:

  • All data is stored locally and can be deleted by uninstalling the app
  • No parental account or registration required
  • Settings can be adjusted at any time
  • Progress can be reset through the app settings

9. Data Security

Since TTKid does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to personal information. All local data is protected by your device's built-in security features.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Compliance

TTKid complies with:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - USA
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - European Union
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California, USA
  • Other applicable children's privacy laws worldwide

13. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our servers. However, you have the right to:

  • Delete all local app data by uninstalling TTKid
  • Reset progress through the app settings
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns
Summary: TTKid is a completely private, offline educational app that does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your child's privacy and safety are our highest priorities.
